Cemu UWP: Wii U games on Xbox Series X|S

An unofficial fork of the Cemu Wii U emulator with a native Direct3D 12 renderer and an Xbox app made for Dev Mode. Mario Kart 8 runs at 1440p or 4K, locked at 60 FPS, with 4 local players on a Series X.

Download the latest build Xbox setup guide Source on GitHub

Free and open source (MPL-2.0). Not affiliated with the Cemu team, Microsoft or Nintendo. Bring your own legally dumped games.

Features

Direct3D 12 renderer

A complete D3D12 backend next to Cemu's Vulkan and OpenGL, so it runs as an Xbox app. Also available in a regular Windows build.

Less stutter

Asynchronous shader compiles, background pipelines, and each level's pipelines are rebuilt on its loading screen the next time you play.

Controller-first launcher

Wii U box art, graphic packs per game, settings and controller pairing, all with a gamepad. LB/RB to switch tabs, A to play.

Up to 4 players

GamePad plus Pro Controllers, Nintendo button layout, remapping, deadzones and controller profiles per player.

GamePad screen

Switch TV and GamePad screens or show the GamePad as an overlay in a corner. Touch cursor for touch-screen games.

Motion, amiibo, Skylanders

Tilt with the right stick or your phone's gyro, amiibo dumps, and a Skylanders portal with several figures at once.

USB drives

Games, saves, shader caches and graphic packs can live on a USB drive, so they survive reinstalls and Dev Mode resets.

Graphic packs and mods

Download the community packs in the app: resolution, FPS, enhancements, mods and translations.

Mario Kart 8 first person

A bundled first person camera pack: cockpit view with Mario's hands on the wheel. Right stick switches views.

How to install on Xbox

  1. Put your Xbox Series X|S in Developer Mode (Microsoft's Dev Mode Activation app).
  2. Download cemu-uwp-x64.zip from the latest build.
  3. Install it through the Xbox Device Portal in your PC's browser.
  4. Copy your dumped Wii U games (and keys.txt if needed) to a USB drive, plug it in and start Cemu.

Full steps and recommended settings: Xbox setup guide. New builds install as updates over the old one.

FAQ

Does it work in Retail mode? No. Emulators need Xbox Developer Mode.

Series S or Series X? Both. The Series X has more headroom for 4K; the Series S runs most games well at lower resolutions.

Where do I get games? Dump your own Wii U discs and digital games with your Wii U. No games or keys are included.

What changed in the latest build? See the changelog.
